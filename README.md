# Assetflow

Assetflow is an IT asset management workspace. It lets teams inventory their equipment, scan QR and barcode labels with a camera, track inventory analytics on a live dashboard, and review an appendable record of every asset-related event.

Assetflow is built to help organizations keep an accurate, always-current view of their physical IT assets and who is using them, reducing the manual effort and guesswork involved in tracking equipment across a team.
